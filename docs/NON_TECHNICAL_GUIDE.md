# Non-Technical Guide

This guide explains the system in simple terms for Deeper Christian Life Ministry, Kwara State branch.

## What is This System?
It is the database and brain for church operations. It stores all church structure, people records, attendance, offerings, and reports.

## Key Concepts
- **Worker**: A person serving in church. This is their profile.
- **User**: A login account tied to a worker.
- **Location**: A local church.
- **Fellowship**: Small group under a location.
- **Program Event**: A scheduled service or meeting.

## What You Can Do
- Register workers and create their login accounts.
- Track attendance and offerings for services.
- Generate reports for leaders.
- Publish announcements for members.

## Who Uses It
- Admins, pastors, and data-entry workers.
